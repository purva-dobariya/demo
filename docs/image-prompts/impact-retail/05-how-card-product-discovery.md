## Subject & scene
An overhead-to-slightly-angled close-up flat-lay of a rich spread of authentic regional Indian products, arranged as if just packed for dispatch or freshly displayed at a producer's own stall: small burlap/jute sacks or brass bowls of turmeric, red chilli powder, and whole spices; a woven bamboo basket of millet-based snacks or grains; a stack of neatly folded hand-block-printed or handwoven textile pieces; one or two pieces of hand-thrown terracotta or brass craftware; and a couple of simple kraft-paper-wrapped parcels tied with jute twine, perhaps with a small handwritten tag (illegible/generic scrawl, not real text) suggesting they're ready to ship. A producer's hand may enter the frame from one side, adjusting or packing one of the items, to keep the shot feeling human rather than like a sterile product-catalogue photo.

## Composition & framing
Landscape orientation, high-angle or 45-degree flat-lay style close-up, filling most of the frame with the products arranged in a naturalistic, slightly irregular cluster (not a rigid grid) atop a rustic wooden table, woven mat, or worn cotton cloth surface. Keep the top strip of frame (background surface, soft shadow) relatively plain to protect the step-number overlay. Shallow-to-moderate depth of field so textures — grain, weave, spice powder grain — are crisp and inviting, giving the sense of tactile, real, high-quality regional goods.

## People
Optional: a single hand and forearm (any gender, adult, Indian skin tone, plain everyday clothing sleeve visible) entering from one edge of frame to adjust or place a product, suggesting a producer mid-preparation — no full face or posed portrait needed in this card.

## Lighting & color
Warm, soft natural light — as if near a window or open doorway — falling diagonally across the spread to bring out genuine texture and color in the spices, grains, and textiles. True-to-life, rich, saturated-but-natural color grading celebrating the actual hues of turmeric yellow, chilli red, undyed cotton, and terracotta — no pre-applied blue or navy tint; the section's dark card treatment and opacity reduction are applied afterward via CSS.

## Mood
Abundant, authentic, tactile, celebratory

## Must avoid
No branded packaging or legible labels/text on any item. No sterile, white-background e-commerce catalogue look — this should feel like a real producer's table, not a studio product shoot. No HDR over-saturation that makes spices look artificial. No AI artifacts in hand/finger anatomy if a hand is included. No non-Indian food/craft items. No baked-in text/numerals/watermark (the "05" is applied by the page itself).
