# Challenges Sticky Panel

## Location on page
- Page: Impact Retail (`Impact Retail.html`)

## Role in the page
This tall sticky panel runs alongside the vertical list of five "Key challenges" (limited market access, low digital visibility, logistics and delivery gaps, packaging and cataloguing gaps, hard onboarding journeys). As the reader scrolls down the challenge list, this single image stays pinned beside it, so it has to function as one visual synthesis of all five frictions at once — a grounded, human scene that makes the abstract bullet points feel like a real, specific place rather than a generic problem statement.

## Display context
- Shape: Portrait, aspect-ratio 3:4
- Recommended output size: ~1500x2000px
- Presentation: Sticky-positioned card at 85% opacity over a black background, dark section overall (deep navy gradient). No caption or text overlay sits on top of this particular image (unlike the reality-img slot), but it reads at reduced opacity against a dark backdrop, so avoid a composition that depends on very dark shadow areas for legibility — mid-tones and enough natural contrast are needed to read through the opacity reduction.

## Subject & scene
A rural artisan or smallholder farmer — mid-40s to 60s — sits cross-legged on the floor of a modest home or open-fronted workshop, tying closed a hand-wrapped parcel of goods (a bundle of block-printed cloth, or a stack of terracotta pots wrapped in old newspaper and jute twine) using string, because proper packaging materials aren't available. Beside them on a worn cotton mat: a basic keypad or entry-level touchscreen phone, a hand-written ledger/notebook with tallies of orders, and a small stack of finished handmade products (baskets, pottery, or folded textiles — unbranded, generic craft forms, not any specific trademarked product). Through an open doorway behind them, a glimpse of an unpaved village lane with no vehicle in sight — visually implying the isolation from reliable pickup and delivery. The setting should read as authentically rural Indian: mud-plastered or exposed-brick wall, a single ceiling bulb or window as the light source, no signage, no branding.

## Composition & framing
Vertical (portrait) composition, medium-to-wide shot taken from a slightly lowered eye-level angle so the subject's hands and the parcel-tying action are the clear focal point in the upper two-thirds of frame, with the doorway/lane visible in softer focus toward the middle-background. Leave the lower portion of the frame relatively simple — mat, floor, edge of the parcel — since there is no caption here but the image will be dimmed by opacity; avoid cramming fine detail into deep shadow at the very bottom edge. Natural documentary lens feel, moderate depth of field (not creamy bokeh — this should read as reportage, with the room legible, not an isolated studio portrait).

## People
One person: an Indian rural producer, weathered hands, plain everyday clothing appropriate to work at home (a faded cotton kurta or saree pallu tucked back for work, sleeves/pallu out of the way), fully absorbed in tying the parcel — not looking at camera, no smile, a look of quiet concentration and routine effort. Gender can be either; a woman artisan works well and continues the site's existing visual language (SHG/artisan focus established elsewhere on this page).

## Lighting & color
Single-source natural light — either window light or an open doorway — falling across the workspace at a low, warm angle suggesting late morning. Real, unretouched shadow falloff, slightly dusty/warm ambient tone typical of an actual rural interior. True-to-life color grading: warm terracottas, muted cottons, worn wood — do NOT pre-tint blue or navy; the section's dark gradient and opacity reduction are applied afterward in CSS.

## Mood
Resourceful, isolated, dignified, quietly strained

## Must avoid
Do not depict all five challenges literally/diagrammatically (no icons, no montage grid) — this is one cohesive human scene. No branded packaging, no visible logos, no smartphone screens showing legible real app UI. No posed "problem stock photo" look (e.g., person looking frustrated at camera). No HDR over-saturation, no glossy studio lighting, no AI artifacts in hands/fingers, no text or watermark baked into the image, no non-Indian setting or ethnicity.
