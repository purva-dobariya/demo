## Role in the page
This image sits in a sticky panel beside the vertical list of five challenge cards: rising customer acquisition costs, high commission-led marketplace models, fragmented fulfilment and logistics, limited access to scalable digital discovery, and operational dependence on a limited number of platforms. As the reader scrolls down the challenge list, this single image stays pinned in view — it must function as a visual summary of "the structural burden of running a D2C brand today," reinforcing all five pain points at once rather than illustrating just one.

- Shape: Portrait, aspect-ratio 3:4 (narrower/taller crop than the reality image above it)
- Recommended output size: ~1500x2000px
- Presentation: Rendered at 85% opacity directly on a dark near-black card background (no gradient/caption overlay on this one — the whole frame stays visible), sitting sticky next to the scrolling challenge list. Because there's no caption to accommodate, compose for full-frame visual interest, but keep it moody/dark enough to sit comfortably beside the dark `.reality` section's other content.

## Subject & scene
An overhead or high-angle shot of a small D2C brand's cluttered dispatch table caught mid-operation: multiple courier company shipping bags and unmarked (generic, no real logos) waybills in different colors fanned across the table — visually implying "fragmented logistics, multiple platforms" — stacked alongside a small ledger or notebook and a calculator showing scribbled commission percentages, and a smartphone displaying several different shopping-app icons (blurred/generic, not real logos) implying dependence on multiple marketplaces. A half-packed box sits in the corner. The overall arrangement should feel like visual shorthand for "juggling too many systems at once" — a founder's messy operational reality, not a tidy flat-lay.

## Composition & framing
Top-down or steep 45-degree overhead angle (flat-lay style but with genuine clutter and depth, not a styled Instagram flat-lay), filling the 3:4 frame edge-to-edge with objects so it reads richly even when small in a sidebar. Slight desaturation and darker exposure suit the dark section it sits in — this should look like it was shot deliberately moodier/lower-key than the daylight reality-portrait image, since it has no gradient help from CSS.

## People
Optional: a single pair of hands (a founder's hands) entering from one edge of frame, sorting through the shipping labels or holding the calculator — hands only, no face needed, to keep focus on the objects/clutter that represents the challenges.

## Lighting & color
Directional, slightly moody lighting — a single overhead lamp or window light from one side creating real shadows between the stacked papers and boxes, rather than flat even studio light. Natural, true color temperature (kraft browns, muted courier-bag colors, white paper) — do not add a blue color cast; keep it neutral/true-to-life so the section's existing dark-navy CSS background and radial glow provide the color mood.

## Mood
Overwhelmed but methodical, operationally messy, real

## Must avoid
Styled, pristine "flat lay influencer" aesthetic; any real, recognizable courier or e-commerce logos/brand marks; legible text on labels, ledgers, or phone screens; overly staged symmetry; HDR oversaturation; AI artifacts in hand anatomy; non-Indian currency, script, or packaging conventions.
