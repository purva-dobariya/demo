## Subject & scene
A close, warm still-life-meets-lifestyle shot of finished D2C brand products — think a small skincare, home-goods, or lifestyle brand's actual product line (unbranded, generic labels) arranged on a simple wooden shelf or table, perhaps with a founder's hand adjusting one bottle or box into place, mid-arrangement rather than a static flat lay. A few products still have simple kraft-paper or minimal packaging design elements (textures, not legible logos) suggesting careful, small-batch branding. This should feel like the "finished result" of the brand's work — proud, tactile, real.

## Composition & framing
Medium-close shot, roughly eye-level or slightly above, 50-85mm equivalent lens with a soft, shallow depth of field so 2-3 products are in crisp focus and the rest recede gently. Landscape 16:10 crop with the products occupying the central-to-right two-thirds of frame, leaving the top-left corner comparatively simple for the badge overlay. A single hand entering from one side to adjust a product adds life without needing a full figure or face.

## People
Optional: a single hand only (the founder's), adjusting or placing a product on the shelf — no face required, keeping full attention on the product arrangement itself.

## Lighting & color
Soft, warm natural light, ideally raking across the products from one side (window light) to bring out texture on labels/packaging without creating harsh glare. True, natural color rendering of the products' actual material colors (glass, kraft paper, muted brand tones) — no blue tint added; let the CSS/section styling carry the brand color separately.

## Mood
