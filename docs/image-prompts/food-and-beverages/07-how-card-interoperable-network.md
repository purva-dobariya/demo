## Role in the page
Card "05," titled "Interoperable Restaurant Network," description "Open discovery and ordering across applications, shared logistics across the network," with bullets on restaurants being visible across participating buyer apps, shareable logistics, and easy access for new buyer apps to restaurant supply. This card is the systemic/network step — it should suggest breadth and connection across the food ecosystem: many restaurants, riders, and orders moving through a shared system, rather than a single storefront moment like the earlier cards.

## Display context
- Shape: 16:9
- Recommended output size: ~1600x900px

## Subject & scene
A wider street-level view of a small food market lane or a cluster of adjoining eateries/food stalls at dusk, with multiple delivery riders parked or arriving at different shopfronts simultaneously, suggesting a whole network operating in parallel rather than a single restaurant. Include varied signage-free shopfronts (generic awnings, no legible brand names), a mix of small dhaba-style eateries and a cloud-kitchen-style unit, and 2-3 riders at different points in the frame (one collecting an order, one departing, one arriving) to convey simultaneity and scale.

## Composition & framing
Wide shot, 24-35mm equivalent, camera at street level or from a slightly elevated vantage (like a first-floor balcony) looking down a food lane, letting depth and repetition (multiple shopfronts, multiple riders) tell the "network" story. Keep the top-left area (sky or upper building line) relatively calm for the numeral overlay.

## People
Multiple small/background figures: 2-3 delivery riders at varying distances, Indian, casual delivery wear (unbranded jackets/helmets), captured mid-action (parking, collecting, riding away) rather than posed; a couple of shopkeepers visible in doorways in the middle distance. No single figure needs to be a sharp macro-detail subject — this card is about the ensemble.

## Lighting & color
Dusk/early-evening light with shopfront lights just starting to switch on, warm mixed practical lighting, true-to-life color — no blue/teal pre-tinting.

## Mood
Networked, alive, bustling, interconnected

## Must avoid
Any real, legible shopfront brand names or delivery-platform logos on riders/bikes; overly tidy or artificially symmetric street layout; AI artifacts in repeated background figures (avoid duplicated faces/clone-stamp look); Western streetscape; empty, deserted-feeling lane that undercuts the "network in motion" idea.
