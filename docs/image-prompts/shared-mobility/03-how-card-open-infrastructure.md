## Role in the page
This is the opening card in the horizontal-scroll "how it works" sequence, illustrating the first step: "Open Mobility Infrastructure" — interoperable ride discovery and fulfilment across multiple applications, where a single integration connects to the wider mobility network and riders can discover services across participating apps while providers keep operational independence. As card #1, it sets the tone for the whole sequence and must read clearly as "one rider, one app, many possible rides" in under a second while scrolling horizontally.

## Subject & scene

The implication is a single small screen opening onto a genuine plurality of vehicles and providers, rather than one closed fleet.

Keep the top-left corner relatively simple and slightly darker in tone (sky, wall, or shaded pavement) to host the "01" numeral overlay. Landscape 16:9 crop with a little headroom above the subject.

## People

## Lighting & color
