## Role in the page
This is the anchor image of the dark "Structural Reality" section, sitting directly beside the headline "India moves millions of commuters daily, yet digital access to transport remains fragmented" and the pull-quote about disconnected systems with limited interoperability. Its caption reads: "A single trip across modes often means switching between three or four different apps." The image (alt text in the markup: "Crowded local train") must visually ground that fragmentation in a real, dense, everyday Indian commuting moment before the reader scans the "Key challenges" list beside it.

## Display context
- Shape: Portrait, aspect-ratio 4:5, capped at max-height 500px
- Recommended output size: ~1600x2000px
- Presentation: Rendered at 84% opacity over a black card background, with a vertical gradient overlay fading from nearly transparent (4% black) at 45% down to 68% black at the very bottom. The caption text ("A single trip across modes often means switching between three or four different apps" + small-caps "The reality on the ground") sits inside a ~20px inset at the bottom of that darkened zone. Keep the bottom quarter of the composition visually simple (platform floor, blurred crowd, soft shadow) — no faces or critical detail in the very bottom 15-20% of frame.

## Subject & scene
A densely packed metro or suburban-rail platform/carriage during evening rush hour in a major Indian city (read as generically Indian — could be Delhi Metro-style or Mumbai suburban-rail-style architecture, but avoid any legible signage naming a real line or station). Commuters of mixed ages stand shoulder to shoulder waiting to board or already packed inside a train doorway — office-goers with laptop bags, a woman in a saree, a man in a factory uniform, a student with a backpack — some glancing at phones, one checking a paper ticket stub, capturing the everyday friction of moving through the system.

## Composition & framing
Portrait-oriented, shot from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle down the platform or across the carriage doorway, so the crowd recedes into soft depth toward the back of frame. Subject mass fills the upper two-thirds of the frame; the lower third should ease into out-of-focus platform flooring, legs, or shadow so the caption gradient has room without cutting across a face. Use a slightly longer lens (70-85mm equivalent) compressed perspective to emphasize density without distorting people at the edges.

## People
A realistic cross-section of Indian daily commuters: 6-10 visible figures of mixed age (20s-50s) and gender, in ordinary work and travel clothing — collared shirts, sarees, kurtas, backpacks, one holding a metro smart-card or paper ticket. Expressions should be neutral-to-tired, mid-motion (stepping, adjusting a bag strap, checking the time) — not posed, not smiling at camera, nobody looking directly into the lens.

## Lighting & color
Cool fluorescent/LED platform lighting mixed with a hint of warm evening light spilling in from a platform opening, true-to-life and slightly desaturated the way real transit-hub lighting looks — do not pre-tint blue or navy; the section's dark overlay and vignette are applied separately in CSS.

## Mood
Dense, hurried, authentic, quietly overwhelming

## Must avoid
Generic Western subway/train imagery; legible real transit-authority signage, line names, or logos; posed "commuters smiling at camera" stock look; anyone staring into the lens; over-bright HDR color grading; motion-blur artifacts that read as AI glitches; empty/sparse platform (must read as genuinely crowded); anything critical placed in the bottom 15-20% of frame where the caption overlays.
