Metro station architecture in the background: stainless-steel gate arrays, platform signage (blurred/illegible), a few other commuters passing through adjacent gates, bright architectural lighting typical of an Indian metro concourse (Delhi Metro/Namma Metro/Chennai Metro style, but not identifiably branded).

## Composition & framing
Medium shot at gate height, camera slightly to the side so both the commuter's phone-tapping hand and the gate mechanism are visible and in focus; background concourse and other commuters softly blurred behind. Landscape 16:9, subject positioned right-of-center so the top-left corner (numeral zone) stays open concourse space, slightly darker/simpler in tone.

## People
One commuter in sharp focus: a young professional (20s-30s, any gender), in smart-casual office wear, hand extended holding a smartphone flat against the gate reader, mid-stride, natural unposed expression, not looking at the camera. One or two other commuters visible passing through adjacent gates further back, out of focus.

## Lighting & color
