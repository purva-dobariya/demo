## Role in the page
This sticky portrait panel sits beside a vertical list of five challenge cards: "Low discoverability," "Fragmented inventory," "Public and private silos," "Inconsistent discovery and booking," and "Limited digital participation." As the reader scrolls down the list, this single image stays pinned, so it must function as a durable visual summary of "tourism supply that exists in the real world but is scattered, disconnected, and hard for a traveller to find" — a small, independent tourism operator working entirely outside any digital booking system.

- Shape: Portrait, aspect-ratio 3:4
- Recommended output size: 1500x2000px
- Presentation: Sticky-positioned panel, displayed at 85% opacity against a very dark navy background with rounded corners and a deep drop shadow. No caption or text overlay sits directly on this image (unlike the reality-img), so detail can extend closer to the edges, but keep the composition self-contained since it will be viewed in isolation for an extended scroll duration.

## Subject & scene
A desert safari operator in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan — a wiry man in his 50s wearing a bright saffron turban and simple white kurta-pyjama — standing beside his camel and a hand-painted wooden signboard advertising "Sunset Desert Safari" in Hindi and English, on the edge of the Thar desert dunes. He holds a paper ledger/notebook and a bunch of loose paper tickets rather than a phone or tablet, visually reinforcing "fragmented, offline inventory." In the background, sparse desert scrub, a distant cluster of similar small operators each with their own camels and hand-lettered signs, no shared branding or standard signage between them — visually conveying disconnected, siloed supply.

## Composition & framing
Medium shot, camera at eye-level with the subject, three-quarter angle so both the man and his camel plus the signboard are legible. Rule-of-thirds placement with the operator slightly right of center, sign and camel filling the left third. Natural depth of field — subject in sharp focus, the cluster of other operators in the distance softly out of focus to suggest scale without clutter. Keep some open sky/dune space in the upper portion for breathing room since the panel is viewed for a long scroll span.

## People
A Rajasthani desert safari guide, approximately mid-50s, weathered sun-lined face, thick moustache, wearing a bright saffron-orange turban (safa) and plain off-white kurta-pyjama, holding a well-worn paper ledger and a handful of loose ticket stubs. Expression: patient, slightly squinting into the sun, not smiling for camera — caught mid-task rather than posed.

## Lighting & color
Late-afternoon desert sun, warm golden light with long shadows across the sand, but not overexposed — retain visible texture in the turban fabric and camel's coat. True desert palette: sandy beige and ochre dunes, saffron turban, deep blue sky. Do not apply any blue/cyan brand tint in the base photo — that treatment is added later via CSS.

## Mood
Independent, resourceful, disconnected, dignified

## Must avoid
No modern digital ticketing kiosks, QR codes, phones, or tablets in frame (this represents the *offline/fragmented* state the copy describes); no touristy staged "camel ride selfie" framing; no oversaturated HDR desert-sunset postcard look; no baked-in text or logos on the signboard beyond simple hand-painted lettering; no AI artifacts in camel anatomy or turban folds; avoid clichéd "exotic Orientalist" styling — this should read as a real small business owner, not a costume.
