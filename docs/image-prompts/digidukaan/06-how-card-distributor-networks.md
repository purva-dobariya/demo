## Subject & scene
Inside or just outside a small-to-mid-size wholesale distributor's godown/warehouse, two or three loaders/warehouse workers are stacking or loading cardboard cartons of generic packaged goods onto a small delivery tempo/mini-truck, while the distributor or warehouse supervisor stands nearby, glancing at a phone or tablet in one hand while overseeing the loading with the other. This should read as a working, slightly dusty, real logistics environment — stacked pallets, a hand-truck/trolley, taped cartons — not a sanitized studio warehouse. The digital device in the supervisor's hand should be a small, secondary detail; the primary visual energy is the physical loading work continuing as it always has.

## Composition & framing
Medium-wide establishing shot capturing the loading activity as the main action, with the supervisor and phone/tablet as a smaller supporting detail within the frame (not the single focal point) — this reinforces "existing systems continue, digital is additive." Slightly elevated or eye-level angle looking across the loading bay. Keep the top-left of the frame relatively uncluttered for the "05" numeral overlay. Moderate depth of field so multiple figures and cartons stay reasonably sharp.

One distributor/supervisor figure, slightly older (40s-50s), in a plain collared shirt, standing to the side holding a phone/tablet and glancing at it while watching the loading. All caught in natural mid-task poses, not posed for camera.

## Lighting & color
