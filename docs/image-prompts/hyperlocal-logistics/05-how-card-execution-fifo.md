## Subject & scene
The pickup counter or loading bay of a busy hyperlocal fulfilment hub / dark store at a peak hour, showing genuine first-in-first-out order: a line of sealed parcels/order bags arranged in numbered or sequential order on a shelf or counter, a staff member calling out or checking off the next parcel in line, and two or three delivery riders queued just behind each other (not clustered randomly) waiting their turn to collect the next order in sequence — visually reinforcing the queue/FIFO concept. A small digital token-number display or a simple printed queue-ticket system could be visible on the counter to reinforce sequencing without needing legible real text.

## Composition & framing
Frame the counter and the queue of parcels/riders together, landscape orientation, ideally with the line of parcels leading the eye from foreground into the middle of the frame, and the riders' queue receding to one side — creating a visual "conveyor of order" feel. Keep the top-left corner (wall, shelving edge, or simple signage backdrop) visually calm for the "04" numeral overlay. Slightly faster energy in body language (a rider already reaching for the next parcel) conveys pace without needing full motion blur.

## People
A hub staff member (20s-40s, any gender), wearing a simple apron or plain uniform shirt, actively handing over or checking a parcel against a handheld scanner; two to three delivery riders in a visible line behind, each in a different plain helmet/jacket color, one already reaching to take his parcel — captured mid-action, purposeful expressions, not posed looking at camera.

## Lighting & color
Bright, practical interior lighting typical of a small fulfilment hub — a mix of overhead fluorescent/LED strip lighting with perhaps one shaft of natural light from a loading-bay door — true, slightly cool-neutral color without being clinical. Do not pre-tint blue; CSS applies the brand-color treatment separately on top of the true photo.

## Mood
Fast-paced, orderly, high-volume, efficient
