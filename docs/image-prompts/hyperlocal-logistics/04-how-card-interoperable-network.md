## Subject & scene
A street-level handoff point — outside a small urban dark-store, apartment gate, or market lane — where a package is being transferred directly from one delivery rider to another, each clearly from a different fleet (distinguished only by different plain helmet/jacket colors and different bike/scooter cargo-box styles, no real brand liveries). Keep the top-left corner (sky, wall, or shopfront awning) simple for the "03" numeral. Slightly faster shutter feel (a touch of motion in a wheel or kickstand) suggests real street activity without full blur.
