## Subject & scene
A modest neighbourhood kirana store at the edge of a bazaar lane: the shopkeeper, a man in his 40s-50s in a plain collared shirt, stands behind his counter holding a basic smartphone up near a handwritten ledger, checking a simple order or stock screen (generic, blurred UI, no legible app branding). Shelves behind him are packed with everyday household goods — soap bars, snack packets, tea boxes, plastic containers — in the slightly chaotic, dense arrangement typical of a real Indian general store, not a styled prop shelf. Through the open shopfront in the middle distance, a delivery rider on a motorbike with an insulated delivery box passes along the lane, softly out of focus, gesturing toward the logistics and gig-economy themes without competing with the main subject.

## Composition & framing
Medium shot from just outside the counter, roughly eye-level with the shopkeeper, camera angled slightly to include both the counter (foreground/midground) and the sliver of street visible through the doorway (background). Vertical 3:4 crop with the shopkeeper positioned in the upper two-thirds of frame and the counter/ledger anchoring the lower third. Some depth-of-field falloff on the street scene behind keeps the eye on the shopkeeper first, the moving rider second.

## People
