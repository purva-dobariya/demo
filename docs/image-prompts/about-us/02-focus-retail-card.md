## Role in the page
This is the opening card in the horizontal-scroll "focus areas" sequence, representing "Retail" — digitisation for kiranas and bazaars, livelihoods for micro producers, and efficiency gains for D2C brands. As card #1 of 4 (Retail, Logistics, Mobility, Financial Services), it needs to instantly read as "everyday Indian retail going digital" so a reader skimming the horizontal track understands the category within a glance before the heading and bullets are even read.

## Display context
- Shape: 16:9
- Recommended output size: ~1600x900px
- Presentation: Rendered at 82% opacity inside a rounded card on a dark blue/cream card background, with a large "01" numeral overlaid in the top-left corner in semi-transparent white with a soft drop shadow — keep the top-left ~15% of the frame relatively simple and not too bright so the numeral stays legible. On hover the image scales up 1.05x, so keep the main subject centered rather than tight against the edges.

## Subject & scene
A small kirana or general-store owner, mid-30s to mid-40s, standing at his shop counter using a basic tablet or handheld device to check stock or fulfil an online order, with densely packed shelves of packaged goods (grains, snacks, household items) behind him. On the counter, a few sealed parcels or bags ready for pickup suggest the store is now also fulfilling digital orders, not just walk-in customers — a visual bridge between traditional retail and the digital layer ONDC provides.

## Composition & framing
Medium shot, roughly chest-height camera angle looking slightly up or level at the shopkeeper, standard-lens perspective (35-50mm equivalent) with moderate depth of field — shopkeeper and counter in sharp focus, shelves behind softly resolved. Landscape 16:9 crop with the shopkeeper positioned right-of-center, leaving calmer negative space in the upper-left for the numeral overlay.

## People
An Indian small-business owner, male or female, mid-30s to mid-40s, wearing everyday work clothing (a simple kurta or collared shirt), sleeves rolled up, engaged and focused on the device rather than posing for the camera — hands mid-motion tapping or scrolling, a natural half-smile or concentrated expression, not a broad camera-facing grin.

## Lighting & color
Warm daylight from a shopfront window or open door, soft and slightly directional, true-to-life color rendering of packaged-goods labels and skin tones — no blue color grading baked in, since the card sits on a light background where brand-blue accents are applied separately via CSS icons and borders.

## Mood
Practical, industrious, quietly optimistic

## Must avoid
Any real, legible product or app brand names/logos; overly glossy "stock photo entrepreneur" posing; studio-lit artificiality; HDR oversaturation; AI artifacts in hands or device screens; non-Indian retail signage, currency, or shop conventions.
