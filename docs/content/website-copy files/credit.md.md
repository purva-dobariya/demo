The Structural Reality

Millions of individuals and businesses continue to face challenges in accessing formal credit.

Common barriers include:

* Lengthy onboarding processes  
* Fragmented lender ecosystems  
* Limited access for first-time borrowers  
* Higher operational complexity across participants

For lenders:

* Multiple integrations increase deployment effort  
* Customer acquisition costs remain significant  
* Reaching underserved borrower segments can be difficult

ONDC is helping create a more connected lending ecosystem that enables broader participation, greater efficiency and improved access to formal credit.
