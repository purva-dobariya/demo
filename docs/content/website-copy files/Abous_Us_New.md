**About ONDC** 

India has built some of the world's most transformative digital public infrastructure.

From identity and payments to data and services, technology has helped bring millions of people into the formal economy and unlock new opportunities for growth.

Commerce is the next chapter.

ONDC is building the digital infrastructure that enables businesses, consumers and service providers to connect and transact through an open network.

For a neighbourhood retailer, it can mean ease of running business. For a restaurant, better margins. For a farmer, access to wider markets. For a startup, the ability to innovate without rebuilding the foundations of commerce or monetizing traffic without having to build the full stack. For a customer, lower prices for the same products and services or access to products and services that weren’t digitized before.

By creating common digital rails for commerce, ONDC is helping make participation easier, competition fairer and innovation more accessible.

The vision is simple: a digital commerce ecosystem where opportunity is determined by value and service, and where every business has the ability to grow.

Body Copy

India has demonstrated the successful adoption of digital infrastructure at population scale through initiatives such as Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, and FASTag.

These systems fundamentally transformed how identity, payments, and digital public services operate across the country.

ONDC builds on this larger digital transformation journey by applying similar network-led principles to commerce.

The broader vision is to help make digital commerce more accessible, interoperable, scalable, and inclusive across India.

  ### **WHY ONDC MATTERS**

India has one of the world's fastest-growing digital economies, yet a significant portion of commerce remains disconnected from organized digital systems.

Many businesses continue to face challenges such as:

* Limited online visibility  
* High platform dependency  
* Restricted market access  
* High customer acquisition costs  
* Limited access to interoperable technology infrastructure

ONDC addresses these challenges through an open network architecture that enables participation across platforms and applications.

The initiative creates opportunities for:

* Businesses to access wider customer bases  
* Buyers to discover greater choice across platforms  
* Startups and technology partners to innovate on open infrastructure  
* Logistics and payment providers to integrate seamlessly  
  ---

  ### **OUR IMPACT**

From ordering food and booking rides to accessing financial services and discovering local businesses, ONDC is helping create a more connected digital economy where participation is open to everyone.

The initiative is contributing towards:

* Opening new opportunities for businesses, workers and service providers to participate in the digital economy  
* Helping consumers access a growing range of products and services across commerce, mobility, travel and financial services  
* Enabling businesses of all sizes to reach customers beyond their immediate geography  
* Supporting innovation through common digital rails that simplify participation for buyers, sellers and ecosystem partners while strengthening India's digital public infrastructure


**OUR ECOSYSTEM**

ONDC functions as a collaborative ecosystem powered by network participants across multiple categories.

The ecosystem includes:

* Buyer applications  
* Seller applications  
* Logistics partners  
* Payment providers  
* Technology service providers  
* Enterprise participants  
* MSMEs and local businesses

This collaborative architecture enables seamless interactions between participants while fostering innovation and competition.
