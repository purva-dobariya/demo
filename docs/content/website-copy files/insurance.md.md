The Structural Reality

Millions of merchants, small businesses and gig workers remain underinsured despite facing everyday operational and livelihood risks.

Common challenges include:

* Limited awareness of insurance products  
* Complex purchasing journeys  
* Higher distribution costs  
* Limited availability of affordable, small-ticket coverage

For businesses:

* Exposure to logistics-related risks  
* Financial losses from theft or damage  
* Limited access to tailored protection products

ONDC is helping create a more accessible insurance ecosystem where protection becomes easier to access, understand and adopt.
