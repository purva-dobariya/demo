The Structural Reality

Millions of Indians want to invest but traditional investment products are often designed around monthly income cycles and larger ticket sizes.

For many individuals, investment products can feel difficult to access due to:

* High minimum investment expectations  
* Limited awareness and financial literacy  
* Products designed around fixed monthly income patterns  
* Lack of access to trusted distribution channels

For distributors:

* Reaching underserved investor segments remains challenging  
* Customer acquisition costs can be significant  
* Expanding access beyond traditional investor bases requires additional effort

ONDC is helping create a more accessible investment ecosystem where small-ticket investing becomes practical, flexible and easier to adopt.
