India's ride-hailing has grown rapidly, but remains concentrated around closed, commission-led platforms.

Drivers operate across multiple apps, navigating inconsistent demand, opaque pricing structures, and high commissions that directly impact earnings. Smaller mobility operators struggle to scale digitally.
