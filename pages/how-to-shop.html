The procedure

How shopping works on ONDC

Four steps, the same on every buyer app on the network.

01

Pick a category

Decide what you are shopping for — groceries, fashion, electronics, beauty, food, mobility, travel or financial services.

02

Choose a buyer app

Every buyer app on the network reaches the same sellers. Pick whichever one you already use, or try a new one.

03

Browse and order

Search, compare and place your order inside that app. Prices, sellers and delivery options come from across the network.

04

Get confirmation and delivery updates

Order confirmation, tracking and delivery updates arrive in the same app you ordered from.
