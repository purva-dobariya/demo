Payment Assurance Program

Settlement discipline, made visible on the network

A voluntary program for Buyer Network Participants (BNPs). Place a refundable Payment Assurance Security Deposit, carry the Payment Assurance Badge, and give Seller Network Participants (SNPs) greater assurance when defined payment-dispute exceptions arise.

Enrol as a BNP → View Badge & Mandate Lists
At a glance

Voluntary — BNPs choose whether to enrol, domain by domain

Refundable — the Security Deposit is returned on withdrawal, with accrued interest for escrow balances

Domain-specific — determined using multiple factors such as transaction volumes, settlement timelines, average order values and reconciliation timelines

Escrow or Bank Guarantee — either instrument may be used to place the required Security Deposit

Published — enrolled BNPs carry the Badge and are shown in the Badge Holder List on this page

Applicable domain — currently being rolled out for Metro; additional domains may be added and this page will be updated

Used only for exceptions — the Security Deposit is used only after a payment dispute is resolved and the liable enrolled BNP still does not pay within the defined timeline

Normal settlements do not change. The Program is a backstop for defined payment-dispute exceptions, not a new settlement mechanism for every transaction.

Why Payment Assurance is needed

Building greater payment confidence on an open network

BNPs and SNPs can transact without a prior bilateral relationship. When a payment dispute remains unresolved, uncertainty over payout can reduce an SNP's willingness to transact freely. The Payment Assurance Program creates a common Network-level backstop for these exceptions.

Delayed payouts

Payment disputes can leave settlement pending even after the underlying transaction has completed.

Limited visibility

SNPs need a clear, published signal of which BNPs have placed a Network-level Payment Assurance Security Deposit.

Network trust

A common assurance mechanism can reduce the need for fragmented bilateral arrangements and strengthen confidence to transact.

For BNPs

Why enrol — and what enrolment involves

Enrolment is voluntary and operates per applicable domain. The Program is currently being rolled out for Metro; additional domains may be added subsequently and this page will be updated accordingly.

What you get

  • A Payment Assurance Badge displayed against your name in the applicable domain
  • Greater visibility and confidence with SNPs that prefer or mandate Badge-holding BNPs
  • One standardized Network-level assurance mechanism instead of multiple bilateral deposit arrangements
  • Interest earned on escrow balances is credited back to you as per the applicable banking arrangement

What the Program asks of you

  • Place the required refundable Payment Assurance Security Deposit through Escrow or a Bank Guarantee
  • Maintain the Security Deposit at or above the required level as it is periodically recalibrated
  • Recoup any shortfall within the defined timeline after notification
  • Share required reconciliation and transaction data for payment-dispute resolution
How to enrol

From application to a live Badge

Apply

Fill the enrolment form with NP and SPOC details, domain, preferred mode of deposit and other required information.

2

Deposit computed

ONDC determines the required Security Deposit using applicable transaction and domain parameters.

3

Fund / submit

Fund the escrow account or complete the Bank Guarantee submission, based on the mode selected in the form.

4

Badge live

Your Badge status is displayed in the Badge Holder List on this page.

Program requirement

Enrolment is voluntary; compliance after enrolment is required. A BNP may need to recoup its Security Deposit if the required threshold increases or if the deposit is used for a dispute payout. If the shortfall is not restored within the defined number of days, the Badge is put on hold and the BNP is marked inactive on the Network until the requirement is fulfilled. This is different from not enrolling or formally disenrolling.

For SNPs

Choose whether to mandate the Program

SNPs do not enrol and do not place a Payment Assurance Security Deposit. An SNP may choose to require the Payment Assurance Badge from the BNPs it transacts with in a domain. If it chooses to mandate the Program, it can submit that intent to ONDC for publication in the Mandate List on this page.

Payout assurance

In exception cases only, the Payment Assurance Security Deposit provides a defined payout backstop if a covered dispute with a Badge-holding BNP is resolved and the liable BNP still does not pay.

Right of refusal, always

You decide which BNPs to transact with. The Badge is additional information, and an SNP may choose whether or not to mandate participation.

Mandate & be listed

Submit the prescribed form for the domain(s) in which you mandate the Program. ONDC will publish your name in the Mandate List on this page.

Payment disputes & payout assurance

The Security Deposit is the last step, not the first

Everyday transactions continue to settle directly between BNPs and SNPs. The Program is used only when a defined payment dispute remains unresolved through the prescribed resolution process.

1

Raise on ONDC CARE

The SNP raises the payment issue with complete order and reconciliation details.

2

Mutual resolution

The NPs first attempt to resolve the issue directly.

3

Escalate

If unresolved, the matter is escalated to ONDC.

4

Resolution issued

ONDC issues a resolution within the defined timeline.

5

Payout assured

If the liable enrolled BNP still does not pay within the defined period, payout is made from its available Security Deposit.

The Badge

What the Badge tells you — and what it does not

The Badge indicates that the BNP has enrolled in the Program and maintains the required Payment Assurance Security Deposit; it is not a broader assessment of the participant.

What it tells you

  • The BNP is enrolled in that domain and maintained the required Payment Assurance Security Deposit as at the date shown
  • Where a payment dispute is resolved against that NP and it does not pay, the amount can be met from its available Security Deposit
  • Status is published per domain and updated on every change

What it does not

  • It is not a certification of the NP's solvency, or of any matter beyond this Program
  • Cover is limited to the amount determined in the dispute resolution and the Security Deposit available at that time
  • It applies only to rare settlement disputes arising from ONDC Network transactions; payouts are governed by the applicable Transaction Level Contract (TLC)
  • Offline or bilateral contracts between BNPs and SNPs are outside the Program and must be settled bilaterally
On Hold

A BNP may appear as "On Hold" in the Badge Holder List. This means its Security Deposit is below the required level and was not restored within the defined timeline. The BNP remains inactive on the Network until the shortfall is recouped. SNPs should check the Badge Holder List on the ONDC website for the latest Badge status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Program

What kind of dispute does this Program cover?

Payment disputes only — where a settlement due from a BNP to an SNP is unpaid or delayed beyond the expected timeline and the NPs are unable to resolve it themselves.

Does this change ordinary settlement between a BNP and an SNP?

No. Normal transactions continue to settle directly between BNP and SNP. The Program is used only for defined exception cases.

Are cancellations, returns, delivery failures or quality issues covered?

Not on their own. These follow the existing order and issue-resolution processes. The Program applies only when such a matter results in an unpaid settlement obligation from a BNP to an SNP.

When is the Payment Assurance Security Deposit used?

Only after the defined dispute process is completed and the liable BNP still does not pay within the applicable timeline. It is a backstop, not the first step.

Is participation in the Program mandatory?

No. BNP enrolment is voluntary. However, once enrolled, the BNP must continue to meet the Program requirements until it formally disenrols.

What if a BNP does not recoup a shortfall in time?

ONDC will notify the BNP and provide the defined period to recoup. If the shortfall is not restored in time, the Badge is put On Hold and the BNP is marked inactive on the Network until the required level is restored.

Does a BNP lose its Security Deposit if it leaves the Program?

No. The Payment Assurance Security Deposit is refundable and adjustable in accordance with the Program terms; it is not a fee.

Can a BNP resume transacting after being marked inactive?

Yes. Once the required shortfall is recouped, the BNP can be reinstated and its Badge status restored in accordance with the Program process.
